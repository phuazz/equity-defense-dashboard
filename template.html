6 Defensive Strategies

S1: Enhanced Stress Signal

Entry: 8D count ≥115 OR (SPX < 200d AND canary bad) → IEF. Exit: SPX > 200d + canary clear + 63d cooldown.

S2: Faber 10-Month SMA

Monthly. SPX > 10M SMA → SPY, else → IEF. Source: Faber (2007).

S3: Dual Momentum (Antonacci)

Monthly. SPY 12M > IEF 12M AND > 0 → SPY, else → IEF.

S4: VIX Term Structure

Daily. VIX/VIX3M > 1.0 for 3 consecutive days (confirmed backwardation) → IEF, else → SPY. 3-day filter eliminates whipsaw from 1-2 day spikes. T+1 execution.

S5a: Composite — IEF (Duration Hedge)

Graduated defence into IEF (7-10Y UST). Benefits from flight-to-quality rallies in rate-cutting crises (2008, 2020). Risk: duration losses if rates rise during equity drawdowns (2022).

S5b: Composite — SHY (Short Duration)

Same signals, same Grad D allocation, but defensive asset is SHY (1-3Y UST, ~2yr duration). Minimal rate risk vs IEF's ~7yr duration. Small flight-to-quality benefit in crises. Avoids the 2022-style double-loss scenario. Data from Jul 2002.
